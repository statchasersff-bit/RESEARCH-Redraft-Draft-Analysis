DATA BEHAVIOR

1. Single year mode
When user selects 2024 or 2025:
- Show only that year’s data.
- All baseline calculations should use only that selected year.
- All strategy rankings, miss risk, round value, and recommendations should be recalculated for that year only.

2. Combined mode
When user selects 2024 + 2025:
- Combine the two seasons into one larger sample.
- Recalculate baselines from the combined sample.
- Recalculate strategy results from the combined sample.
- Do not average the two years together equally.
- Weight by team/sample count.
- Example:
  If 2024 has 20,000 teams and 2025 has 40,000 teams, 2025 should naturally have more influence.

3. Compare Years mode
When user selects Compare Years:
- Do not simply combine seasons.
- Show year-over-year differences.

For each strategy, show:
- 2024 playoff rate
- 2025 playoff rate
- change in playoff rate
- 2024 championship rate
- 2025 championship rate
- change in championship rate
- 2024 top-3 PF rate
- 2025 top-3 PF rate
- change in top-3 PF rate
- 2024 sample
- 2025 sample
- sample quality
- trend label

TREND LABELS
Create trend labels:

Improving:
- playoff rate increased by at least +3.0pp
AND sample size is reliable in both years

Declining:
- playoff rate decreased by at least -3.0pp
AND sample size is reliable in both years

Stable:
- playoff rate change is between -3.0pp and +3.0pp
AND sample size is reliable in both years

Noisy:
- either year has insufficient sample size

Volatile:
- playoff rate moved by at least 5.0pp, but sample size is only directional/reliable, not strong/elite

Use “pp” for percentage points. Do not use “%” when describing differences.

SAMPLE QUALITY RULES
Use these labels:

Elite Sample:
- sample >= 5,000

Strong Sample:
- sample >= 1,000

Reliable:
- sample >= 250

Limited Sample:
- sample >= 50

Too Small:
- sample < 50

For year-over-year comparison, the comparison quality should be based on the weaker of the two years.

Example:
2024 sample = 900, 2025 sample = 4,000
Comparison quality = Reliable, because 2024 is below 1,000 but above 250.

IMPORTANT LOW SAMPLE RULE
Hide rows where either year has sample < 50 by default.

Add toggle:
“Show low-sample trends”

When enabled, show them but mark as “Too Small — directional only.”
