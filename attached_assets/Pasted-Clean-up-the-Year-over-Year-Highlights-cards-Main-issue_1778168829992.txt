Clean up the Year-over-Year Highlights cards.

Main issues:
- Too much text inside each card
- Years/samples are hard to scan
- Badges like “Mid” and “High” are unclear
- Card titles feel inconsistent
- The cards need to feel more like executive summary insights

Update the section as follows:

1. Rename section header
Use:
“Year-over-Year Highlights”

Add a small subtitle:
“Biggest strategy trends across selected seasons.”

Keep baseline shift on the right, but make it smaller:
“Baseline shift: 0.0pp”

2. Simplify each card structure
Each card should follow this layout:

Top row:
- Icon
- Insight label
- Sample quality badge

Middle:
- Strategy name
- Main metric, large

Bottom:
- Compact season trend row
- Short explanation

3. Replace confusing labels
Replace:
- “Mid” with “Medium Confidence”
- “High” with “High Confidence”

Or better:
Use sample quality:
- Strong Sample
- Reliable
- Limited Sample

4. Make the season data visual
Instead of paragraph text like:
“2023: 48.1% · 2024: 59.3% · 2025: 54.1%”

Use compact chips:

2023 48.1% | 2024 59.3% | 2025 54.1%

Each year/value pair should be in a small pill or mini stat box.

5. Hide sample counts by default
Do not show:
“5,313 (2023) · 3,632 (2024) · 8,648 (2025)”

inside the main card body.

Instead:
- show total sample only
- put yearly sample counts in tooltip or expandable details

Example:
“n = 17,593 teams”

Tooltip:
2023: 5,313
2024: 3,632
2025: 8,648
